I am Jayavarman VII, leader of Khmer. We Empire rule mainland Southeast Asia from the magnificent city of Angkor between the ninth and fifteenth centuries. Grand Barays names the enormous rectangular reservoirs of Angkor, the West Baray alone holds tens of millions of cubic metres, whose store monsoon water irrigate the rice fields through the dry season and sustaine an immense population. We field the Domrey and build the Prasat. I open with 3 citizens, one warrior and 2 scouts.
