I am Bayan, khagan of the Avars. My horsemen brought the stirrup into Europe, and with it a charge no shield wall had ever felt. For two hundred years Byzantium counted its safety in gold paid to me, and every coin went into the Ring, the great fortress hoard at the heart of my realm. When my envoys wanted more, they simply asked, and Constantinople found the money. Ring of the Avars is that hoard still growing. Tribute and plunder flow inward, my cavalry rides harder and strikes faster than any neighbour, and raiding pays half again for those who do it well. What we cannot tax, we take. The Avar Lancer is the point of the spear; charge him home before their lines are set. And raise the Hring itself, so everything your riders take has somewhere safe to sleep. Strike, seize, ride home; the Ring grows either way.
