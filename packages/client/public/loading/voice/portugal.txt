I am Henry the Navigator, leader of Portugal. Tiny Portugal, secure on the Atlantic edge of Iberia, pioneere the European Age of Exploration. Casa da Índia is the royal trading house in Lisbon that manage the crown's overseas commerce, the spices, gold and goods flowing in from Africa and Asia, as a state monopoly. We field the Nau and build the Feitoria. I open with 2 citizens, one warrior and 2 scouts.
