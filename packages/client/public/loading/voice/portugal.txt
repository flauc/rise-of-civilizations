I am Henry, prince of Portugal, and I never captained the voyages that made me the Navigator. My work was patience: at Sagres I gathered pilots, mapmakers and shipwrights, and year after year I sent captains past the capes that legend said no ship survived. They came back, and each one brought back more of the map. I died before India was reached, but every league of that sea road was charted first in my house. The Casa da India is the counting-house of an ocean. Every spice, every cargo from Africa to the Indies passes through my crown's ledgers in Lisbon. Gold pours from my trade routes, my coastal cities thrive, and my ships sail faster than any rival's. Load the Nau deep and send her where the profit lies farthest, and plant the Feitoria on every foreign shore: a warehouse, a fortress and a flag, all in one building.
