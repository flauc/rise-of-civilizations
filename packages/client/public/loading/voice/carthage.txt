I am Hannibal of Carthage. As a boy I swore undying enmity against Rome, and I kept the oath: I led my army, elephants and all, over the Alps into Italy itself, and at Cannae I destroyed the greatest host Rome ever fielded. For fifteen years I made war in their homeland unbeaten. Phoenician Heritage is the blood of Tyre in our veins, the seafaring and the shrewdness of the mother city grown greater than she ever was. Carthage is a merchant republic: our sea lanes pay for our wars, our trade routes run heavy with silver, and our gold buys the finest horsemen of Numidia. The Carthaginian War Elephant breaks formations by its mere coming; drive it at their centre. And dig a Cothon, the ringed harbour of Carthage, so your fleet is built faster than the enemy can sink it.
