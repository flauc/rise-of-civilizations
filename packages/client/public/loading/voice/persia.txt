I am Cyrus, King of Kings, founder of Persia. In a single generation I raised the largest empire the world had yet seen, from the Aegean to the Indus, and I ruled it not with terror but with tolerance: every people kept its gods, its tongue, its customs, and served me the more gladly for it. Satrapies is my machinery of empire. Each province answers to a satrap, each satrap to my inspectors, the Eyes of the King, and tribute rolls down the Royal Road into my treasuries. Persia grows rich and learned on what a hundred nations bring, and my infantry march with imperial discipline. The Immortals never number fewer than ten thousand; fill each fallen man's place before the enemy sees the gap. And plant a Pairidaeza, my walled paradise garden, wherever your cities need beauty and rest.
