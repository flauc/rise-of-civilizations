I am Brian Boru, leader of Gaelic Ireland. We is a land of many competing kingdoms bound by a share language, law and culture, occasionally unite under a high king. High Kingship names the conteste overlordship of all Ireland, the Ard Rí, that ambitious kings like Brian Boru claime and briefly make real, rising above the patchwork of rival kingdoms. We field the Gallowglass and build the Round Tower. I open with 2 citizens, 2 warriors and one scout.
