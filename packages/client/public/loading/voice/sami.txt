I am Noaidi Council, leader of Sámi. We are the indigenous people of the European far north, across what is now northern Norway, Sweden, Finland and Russia. People of the Eight Seasons reflects the Sámi reckoning of the year not by four seasons but by eight, each ty to a stage in the cycle of reindeer herding, calving, migration, rutting and the rest. We field the Ski Raider and build the Siida Camp. I open with 2 citizens, 2 ski raiders and one scout.
