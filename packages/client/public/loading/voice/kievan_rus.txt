I am Yaroslav, leader of Kievan Rus. We is the first great East Slavic state, founde when Norse Varangian traders and warriors settle among the Slavs along the rivers between the Baltic and the Black Sea. Lavra names the highest rank of Orthodox monastery, such as the Kyiv Caves Lavra with our catacomb chapels and revere relics. We field the Druzhina and build the Lavra. I open with 2 citizens, one warrior and 2 scouts.
