I am Yaroslav, whom the chronicles call the Wise, Grand Prince of Kyiv. My forefathers came down the rivers as Varangian traders, and on the road from the Varangians to the Greeks we built a realm. I raised Saint Sophia in Kyiv, set our law down in writing, and wed my daughters to the kings of France, Norway and Hungary. Lavra is the great monastery, like the Kyiv Caves where monks pray in candlelit catacombs. Since we took the faith from Byzantium, these houses have written our chronicles and painted our icons. Faith and culture pour from them, the very forests yield devotion, and the river road pays gold on every route. My Druzhina, the sworn retinue at my side, breaks whatever the steppe sends against us. Build the Lavra in your cities and let the monks make our name eternal.
