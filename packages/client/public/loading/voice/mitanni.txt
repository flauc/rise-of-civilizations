I am Tushratta, leader of Mitanni. We is a kingdom of the Hurrian people in northern Mesopotamia and Syria, a major Bronze Age power from around 1500 to 1300 BCE that rival Egypt and the Hittites. Maryannu names the elite chariot-warrior aristocracy that forme the striking heart of the Mitanni army, a noble caste defined by the ownership and mastery of the war chariot. We field the Maryannu Chariot and build the Kikkuli Stables. I open with 2 citizens, one warrior and 2 scouts.
