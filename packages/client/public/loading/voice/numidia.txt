I am Masinissa, king of Numidia. I began as a chief among quarrelling Berber tribes and ended a king whom Rome itself courted. At Zama my riders circled Hannibal's flanks and helped bring Carthage down forever. Then I did the harder thing: I taught a nation of herdsmen to plough, and made my kingdom a granary. Rome called me its friend, and I made certain the friendship paid in grain and in glory. Masaesyli Horse is my people's gift. We ride without bridle, guiding the horse by voice alone. Our cavalry strike harder, move farther and see beyond any rival, our mounts recover overnight, and our fields feed everyone. Send my Numidian Cavalry to sting, scatter and vanish before the enemy can turn about. Raise the Royal Horse Market and every city will breed the swiftest horses the Mediterranean has ever seen.
