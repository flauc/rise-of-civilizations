I am Croesus, leader of Lydia. We commande western Anatolia from our capital Sardis, grown rich on the gold-bearing silt of the river Pactolus. Coinage marks Lydia's world-changing innovation: around 600 BCE our kings struck the first standardise stamped coins from electrum, the natural gold-silver alloy wash down by the Pactolus, guaranteeing weight and purity with the royal seal. We field the Heavy Cavalry and build the Mint. I open with 2 citizens, one warrior and 2 scouts.
