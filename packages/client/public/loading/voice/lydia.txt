I am Croesus of Lydia, and my very name means wealth. The river Pactolus washes gold through my capital Sardis, and men measure their fortunes against mine. An oracle told me a great empire would fall if I marched on Cyrus. It fell; it was my own. Learn from me: count no man happy until the end is known. Coinage is my gift to the world. My mint struck the first true coins from electrum, the river's own alloy of gold and silver, weight and purity sworn by the royal seal, and trade itself was transformed: portable, trusted, anonymous value. Lydia gathers gold faster than any kingdom alive. Spend it well. Heavy Cavalry bought with Sardian gold breaks armies raised on promises, and a Mint in every city turns your markets into rivers of coin. Wealth is a weapon; I only drew it too late.
