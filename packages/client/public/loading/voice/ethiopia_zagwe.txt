I am Lalibela, king of the Zagwe, and I did not build my churches: I freed them. Eleven churches carved downward out of living volcanic rock, a new Jerusalem in the highlands for a people cut off from the old one. Pilgrims walk their trenches eight centuries on, and the prayers have never stopped. Aksumite Legacy is the mantle we carry, the faith and kingship of old Aksum restored in a new line. Devotion is our engine: Ethiopia overflows with faith and culture, and that faith can be spent directly on your works, turning prayer into finished stone as my masons did at Roha. The Oromo Cavalry guards the mountain approaches at speed; the highlands defend those who ride them. And hew the Rock-Hewn Church wherever you settle, for what is carved from the mountain cannot be burned.
