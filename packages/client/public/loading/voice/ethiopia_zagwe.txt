I am Lalibela, leader of Ethiopia (Zagwe). We rule the Christian Ethiopian highlands in the twelfth and thirteenth centuries, claiming descent from the older kingdom of Aksum. Aksumite Legacy ties the Zagwe to the venerable Christian kingdom of Aksum, whose mantle of faith and kingship we consciously claime to inherit. We field the Oromo Cavalry and build the Rock-Hewn Church. I open with 2 citizens, one warrior and 2 scouts.
