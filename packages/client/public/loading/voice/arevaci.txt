I am Caros of Segeda, war leader of the Arevaci. When Rome marched on our Celtiberian highlands I led twenty thousand of the tribes out to meet them, and on the feast day of Vulcan, when they believed no one would fight, we ambushed the legions and cut down thousands. My people are the folk of Numantia, the fortress town that, when all was lost, chose fire and death over a Roman chain. Spirit of Numantia is that unbending will. Our workshops labour harder, our melee warriors strike harder, and every new town we found raises its walls before its houses; we build nothing we cannot defend. Send the Celtiberian Warrior against them, stubborn fighters bred in the high country, and raise the Murallas de Numancia; behind those walls a town of herdsmen held the masters of the world at bay for twenty years.
