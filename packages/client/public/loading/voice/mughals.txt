I am Akbar, leader of Mughal Empire. The Mughal Empire, founde by descendants of Timur and Genghis Khan, unite most of the Indian subcontinent under a wealthy, sophisticate and remarkably tolerant dynasty. Padishah is the lofty imperial title of the Mughal emperors, 'master king', proclaiming a sovereignty above mere kings, heir to both Timur and the Persian tradition of divine kingship. We field the Mughal Sowar and build the Red Fort. I open with 2 citizens, one warrior and 2 scouts.
