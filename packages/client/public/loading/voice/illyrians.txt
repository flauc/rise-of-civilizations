I am Teuta, leader of Illyrians. We are a cluster of warlike, seafaring peoples along the eastern Adriatic coast, whose swift ships make them notorious pirates. Adriatic Pirates names the Illyrian raiders whose swift ships preye on the merchant traffic of the Adriatic so relentlessly, and under Queen Teuta so brazenly, that we drew the wrath of Rome. We field the Liburnian and build the Gradina. I open with 2 citizens, one warrior and 2 scouts.
