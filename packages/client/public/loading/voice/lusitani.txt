I am Viriathus, leader of Lusitani. We are a fierce people of western Iberia, in what is now Portugal, who wage a long and brilliant guerrilla war against the might of Rome. Concursare is the Roman term for the hit-and-run skirmishing, darting in to strike and wheeling away before the legions could close, with which Viriathus baffle and bled army after army. We field the Falcata Warrior and build the Castro. I open with 2 citizens, 2 warriors and one scout.
