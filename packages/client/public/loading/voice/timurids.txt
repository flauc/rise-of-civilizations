I am Tamerlane, leader of Timurids. Timur, known in the West as Tamerlane, carve out a brutal empire across Central Asia and Persia from his capital Samarkand in the late fourteenth century. Sword of Islam reflects the self-image Timur cultivate as a holy conqueror smiting the enemies of the faith, however ruthless and self-serving the wars he wage in that name. We field the Timurid Siege Train and build the Registan. I open with 2 citizens, 2 warriors and one scout.
