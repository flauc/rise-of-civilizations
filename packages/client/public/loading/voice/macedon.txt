I am Alexander of Macedon. My father Philip forged the finest army of the age; I took it to the ends of the earth. Persia, Egypt, Babylon, the Indus: I crossed them all before my thirty-third year, and I never lost a battle. Aristotle taught me to think like a Greek. The world taught me to want all of it. Hellenistic Fusion is my design: Greek and Persian made one realm. I married into Bactria, dressed as a king of kings, and enrolled the conquered into my ranks. My soldiers of every kind train faster and march out spirited and seasoned, hammer and anvil in a single hand. Send the Hypaspists in first, the shield-bearers who go wherever the fighting is worst, and found the Basilikoi Paides, the school of royal pages, so each generation of officers is forged before it is needed.
