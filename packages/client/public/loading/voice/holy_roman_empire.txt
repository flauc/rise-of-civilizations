I am Barbarossa, leader of Holy Roman Empire / Germany. We is a sprawling, decentralise patchwork of German principalities, bishoprics and free cities, the political heart of medieval central Europe under an electe emperor. Free Imperial Cities names the self-governing towns that answere to no lord but the distant emperor and so become hothouses of trade, craft and civic pride. We field the Landsknecht and build the Hansa. I open with 2 citizens, 2 warriors and one scout.
