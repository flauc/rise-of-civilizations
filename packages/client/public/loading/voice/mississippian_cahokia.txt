I am Great Sun, leader of Mississippian (Cahokia). Cahokia, near modern St. Mound Builders names the defining achievement of the Mississippian culture: the great earthen platform mounds, raise basketload by basketload, that bore the temples and the houses of the chiefs and make the sacred order of the cosmos visible on the land. We field the Cahokian Warrior and build the Earthwork Mound. I open with 2 citizens, 2 warriors and one scout.
