I am Lars Porsena, leader of Etruscans. We dominate central Italy before the rise of Rome, organise in a loose league of wealthy city-states from Tuscany to the Po valley. Twelve Cities names the Etruscan League, a religious and political confederation of our leading cities that met at a share sanctuary. We field the Etruscan Hoplite and build the Tumulus. I open with 2 citizens, 2 warriors and one scout.
