I am Mehmed II, leader of Ottomans. We rise from a small Anatolian principality to rule three continents for six centuries. Great Bombard recalls the enormous cannon, including the monstrous 'Basilica' bombard, that Mehme II commissione to shatter the ancient triple walls of Constantinople, walls that have defy besiegers for a thousand years. We field the Janissary and build the Grand Bazaar. I open with 2 citizens, 2 javelineers and one scout.
