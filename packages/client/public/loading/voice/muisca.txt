I am Zipa, leader of Muisca. We of the high plateau around modern Bogotá forme a confederation of chiefdoms rich in gold, emeralds and salt. El Dorado, 'the Gilde One', is the Spaniards' name for the Muisca ritual in which a newly chosen chief, coate in gold dust, is rafte out onto Lake Guatavita to cast offerings of gold and emeralds into our waters. We field the Guecha Warrior and build the Salt Temple. I open with 2 citizens, 2 warriors and one scout.
