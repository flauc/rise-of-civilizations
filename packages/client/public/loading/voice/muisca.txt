I am the Zipa, lord of Bacata on the cold high plateau. When I took power I was covered in gold dust and rafted onto the sacred lake at Guatavita, and I gave the gold back to the water. Strangers will garble that rite into a legend and die in the jungles chasing it. Let them come; the mountains will collect the toll. El Dorado, they will call me: the gilded man. Let them chase the myth; I keep the truth. My goldsmiths, my emeralds, and my salt make the highlands rich beyond any raider's dream, and every offering binds the gods closer to my people. The lake keeps what it is given, and it gives back favor. The Guecha Warrior holds the passes into my plateau, and the Salt Temple turns white rock into steady wealth. Trade what the earth gives; drown the rest in the lake, where no thief can follow.
