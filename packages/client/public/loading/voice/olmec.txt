I am Council, leader of Olmec. We of Mexico's Gulf coast are regarde as the 'mother culture' of Mesoamerica, flourishing from around 1200 to 400 BCE. Mother Culture names the Olmec role as the wellspring of Mesoamerican civilization: the gods, the ballgame, the calendar and the artistic motifs that the Maya, Zapotec and Aztec would all inherit appear to begin with them. We field the Olmec Spearman and build the Colossal Head. I open with 2 citizens, 2 warriors and one scout.
