I am Minos, leader of Minoans. We of Crete build Europe's first advanced civilization, flourishing from around 2000 to 1450 BCE. Thalassocracy, Greek for 'rule of the sea', is the word later writers use for the Minoan command of the Aegean, exercise through fleets, trade and cultural prestige rather than armies. We field the Minoan Bireme and build the Labyrinth Palace. I open with 2 citizens, one warrior and 2 scouts.
