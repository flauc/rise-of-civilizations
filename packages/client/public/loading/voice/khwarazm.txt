I am Ala ad-Din Muhammad II, leader of Khwarazm. We dominate Central Asia and Persia in the early thirteenth century, our wealth build on the great oasis cities of the Amu Darya. Shahs of Khwarazm names the dynasty that, in a single generation, raise a sprawling and wealthy empire across Central Asia and Iran, a rise as dazzling as our destruction is complete. We field the Khwarazmian Lancer and build the Gurganj Bazaar. I open with 2 citizens, one warrior and 2 scouts.
