I am Harald Hardrada. At fifteen I bled at Stiklestad; then I fought for the emperor in Constantinople, leading the Varangian Guard from Sicily to the Holy Land, and came home with a shipload of gold to take the crown of Norway. They call me the last of the Vikings. Let every coast say the name softly. Knarr is the broad cargo ship that carries our other face: trader, settler, explorer. We grew rich by sail as much as by sword. My ships run faster than any rival's, gold follows every voyage, and an undefended coast is an invitation I always accept. Strike from the Norse Longship where no army stands waiting, take what you wish, and be gone with the tide. Then raise the Stave Church, timber towering like a ship set on end, so even our prayers smell of the sea.
