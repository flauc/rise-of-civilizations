I am Harald Hardrada, leader of Norse. We of Scandinavia burst onto the wider world in the Viking Age, from the late eighth to the eleventh century, as raiders, traders, explorers and settlers. Knarr names the broad-beame Norse cargo ship, the trader's counterpart to the longship, that carry wares, livestock and colonists across the open North Atlantic. We field the Norse Longship and build the Stave Church. I open with 2 citizens, one warrior and 2 scouts.
