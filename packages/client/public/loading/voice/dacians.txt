I am Decebalus, leader of Dacians. We of the Carpathian mountains, rich in gold, build a powerful kingdom that twice fight the full might of Rome under our last king, Decebalus. Gold of the Carpathians names the rich mountain mines that funde the Dacian kingdom and make it so tempting a prize that Rome's conquest of it filled the imperial treasury and paid for monuments back home. We field the Falxman and build the Murus Dacicus. I open with 2 citizens, 2 warriors and one scout.
