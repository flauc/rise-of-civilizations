I am Balaputra, leader of Srivijaya. We is a Sumatran maritime empire that, from the seventh to the thirteenth century, control the vital Strait of Malacca through which all trade between India and China have to pass. Our Maritime Mandala: +18% gold; coastal cities +2 gold. We field the Jong and build the Candi. I open with 3 citizens, one warrior and 2 scouts.
