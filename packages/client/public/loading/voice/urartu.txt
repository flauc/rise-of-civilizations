I am Sarduri II, leader of Urartu. We is a mountain kingdom around Lake Van in the Armenian highlands, the great northern rival of Assyria from the ninth to the sixth century BCE. Kingdom of Van names Urartu's heartland around our great lake and our citadel capital of Tushpa, hewn into a towering rock above the water. We field the Urartian Charioteer and build the Fortress of Van. I open with 2 citizens, one warrior and 2 scouts.
