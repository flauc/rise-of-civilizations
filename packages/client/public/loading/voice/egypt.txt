I am Hatshepsut, leader of Egypt. We unify around 3100 BCE along the thin green ribbon of the Nile, we endure for some three thousand years, longer than the span separating us from our decline. Iteru is the Egyptian word for the Nile, the river whose behaviour the whole civilization is build to read and exploit. We field the Maryannu Chariot and build the Obelisk. I open with 3 citizens, one warrior and 2 scouts.
