I am Cyaxares, leader of Median Empire. We are an Iranian people who unite the highland tribes and, ally with Babylon, destroye the Assyrian Empire at the fall of Nineveh in 612 BCE. Horse Lords reflects the Medes' fame as breeders and riders of horses, lords of the lush grasslands of Media that produce the celebrate Nisaean mounts. We field the Median Lancer and build the Royal Stable. I open with 2 citizens, one warrior and 2 scouts.
