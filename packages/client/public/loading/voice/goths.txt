I am Theodoric, leader of Goths. The Goths, a Germanic people from the lands north of the Black Sea, playe a leading role in the fall of the Western Roman Empire. Foederati names the status under which Gothic warriors first entere the Roman world: ally troops settle within Our empire and bound to serve in our armies. We field the Gothic Rider and build the Wagon Fort. I open with 2 citizens, one warrior and 2 scouts.
