I am Bumin Qaghan, leader of Göktürks. We is the first state in history to call our people 'Türk', a vast steppe empire that at our height in the sixth and seventh centuries stretch from Manchuria to the Black Sea. Sky Father invokes Tengri, the supreme heaven-god of the Turkic and Mongol peoples, from whom the khagan hold his mandate to rule 'all under the eternal blue sky'. We field the Turkic Lancer and build the Stone Stele. I open with 2 citizens, one warrior and 2 scouts.
