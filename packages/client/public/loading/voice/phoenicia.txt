I am Dido, leader of Phoenicia. We are the master mariners and merchants of the ancient Mediterranean, sailing from our narrow Levantine coast to every shore between Lebanon and the Atlantic. Mediterranean Colonies recalls the chain of trading posts and daughter-cities the Phoenicians strung along every coast, from Cyprus and Sicily to Gades beyond the Pillars of Hercules. We field the Phoenician Bireme and build the Cothon. I open with 2 citizens, one warrior and 2 scouts.
