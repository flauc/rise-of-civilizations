I am Mansa Musa, leader of Mali. We Empire dominate West Africa from the thirteenth to the sixteenth century, growing fabulously rich on control of the trans-Saharan gold and salt trade. Sahel Merchants recalls the great camel caravans that carry Malian gold north across the desert and bring back the salt that the gold-rich south so badly lacke. We field the Mandekalu Cavalry and build the Suguba. I open with 2 citizens, one warrior and 2 scouts.
