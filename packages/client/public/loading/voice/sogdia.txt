I am Divashtich, lord of Panjikent, last prince of free Sogdia. My people never built an empire; we built something better, a web of merchant colonies stretching from Samarkand to the gates of China. Our tongue became the common language of the Silk Road itself, and where armies could not pass, our caravans went singing. We are the Lords of the Silk Road. Sogdia grows rich beyond its rivals, and every trade route we send out carries more gold and more goods than anyone else's, for the middleman always eats first. Samarkand and Bukhara never conquered the world; they simply sold it everything it wanted. My Sogdian Cavalry guard the caravans and cut raiders down before they reach the road. Build Caravanserais along every route, welcome all traders under their arches, and let the world's wealth stop at our door.
