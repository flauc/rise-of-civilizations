I am Divashtich, leader of Sogdia. We are the great merchant middlemen of the Silk Road, an Iranian people whose oasis cities like Samarkand and Bukhara commande the trade routes of Central Asia. Lords of the Silk Road names the Sogdian network of merchant colonies that stretch from our Central Asian oasis cities all the way to China, carrying silk, silver, ideas and faiths. We field the Sogdian Cavalry and build the Caravanserai. I open with 2 citizens, one warrior and 2 scouts.
