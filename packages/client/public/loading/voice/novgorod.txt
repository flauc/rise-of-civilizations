I am Alexander Nevsky, prince of Novgorod. On the Neva I broke the Swedes, and on the frozen lake at Peipus I drew the Teutonic knights onto spring ice and watched it swallow them. Yet I serve a strange master: a republic. The citizens of Novgorod hire their princes and dismiss them at the ringing of a bell, and their fur trade reaches from the Hansa to the Urals. Fur Republic is that wealth. Gold flows from the coasts and along every trade route, for the northern forests are full of sable and squirrel, the German merchants keep their kontor inside our walls, and the traders of the Volkhov know the price of every pelt to the last coin. Loose the Ushkuinik along the rivers to raid and trade in the same breath, and hang the Veche Bell in every city; while it rings, the people rule, and a free city fights like no subject ever will.
