I am Aguda, leader of Jurchen (Jin). We rise under Aguda to overthrow our Khitan overlords and then conquere northern China from the Song, founding the Jin dynasty in the twelfth century. Meng'an-Mouke names the Jurchen military-social organisation, a system of warrior-households groupe into units of a thousand and a hundred that both raise the armies and settle the conquere land. We field the Iron Pagoda and build the Meng'an Garrison. I open with 2 citizens, one warrior and 2 scouts.
