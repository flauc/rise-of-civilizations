I am Sargon, leader of Akkad. Around 2334 BCE Sargon, a cup-bearer who seize power, welde the squabbling Sumerian city-states into the first true empire in history, ruling from the city of Akkad, a capital so thoroughly lost that archaeologists have never find it. Sons of Sargon evokes the dynasty that first turne conquest into empire. We field the Sargonic Guard and build the Palace Archive. I open with 3 citizens, 2 warriors and one scout.
