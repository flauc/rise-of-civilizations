I am Kupe, leader of Māori. We are the last great wave of Polynesian migration, reaching the previously uninhabite islands of Aotearoa, New Zealand, around the thirteenth century. Mana is the spiritual power, authority and prestige that a person, group or place holds in Māori belief, earne through ancestry, deeds and especially success in war, and lost through defeat or dishonour. We field the Toa and build the Pā. I open with 2 citizens, 2 toas and one scout.
