I am Suppiluliuma, leader of Hittites. We build an empire from our fortify Anatolian capital Hattusa, flourishing from roughly 1650 to 1180 BCE and rising to rival New Kingdom Egypt as a Bronze Age superpower. Iron of Hatti recalls that Anatolia lay at the cradle of ironworking, a craft the Hittites and our neighbours practise while iron is still rarer and more prize than gold. We field the Hittite Chariot and build the Storm Temple. I open with 2 citizens, one warrior and 2 scouts.
