I am Suppiluliuma, Great King of Hatti. I found my kingdom in ashes and rebuilt it into a power that made Pharaoh himself uneasy. From the stone fortress of Hattusa my armies crossed the plateau in every direction, and when Egypt's widowed queen sought a new husband, it was to my house she wrote. The Bronze Age has two great thrones, and one of them is mine. Let other kings hoard gold; my smiths hammer iron, the metal princes beg for and I ration like water in a drought. Iron of Hatti keeps the forges of Hattusa burning day and night, and puts hardened weapons into trained hands faster than any rival can muster. Three men to a chariot, driver, shield and spear, thunder across the plain. The Hittite Chariot shatters battle lines, and the Storm Temple keeps the god of tempests fighting at our side.
