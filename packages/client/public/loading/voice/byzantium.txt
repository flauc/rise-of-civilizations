I am Justinian, emperor of the Romans, ruling from Constantinople long after old Rome fell. I set the whole of Roman law in order in one great code, raised Hagia Sophia in five years, and sent Belisarius to win back Africa and Italy. Solomon, I said beneath that dome, I have outdone you. Taxis means order, and order is how we govern and how we war. My officers fight by the book because I wrote the book. Learning, art, faith and gold advance in measured ranks, and my soldiers, foot and horse alike, strike with a discipline that valour alone cannot match. The Byzantine Cataphract, man and horse sheathed in iron, delivers the charge exactly as the manual commands. And build the Hippodrome; give the people their races and their factions, and they will forgive an emperor almost anything.
