I am Jaya Indravarman IV, leader of Champa. We is a string of Hindu kingdoms along the coast of central Vietnam, a seafaring and trading civilization that endure for over a thousand years. Lords of the Sea reflects the Cham mastery of the South China Sea coast, whose ports live by trade and whose ships, when trade is thin, turne to piracy along the busy maritime routes. We field the Cham Raider and build the My Son Tower. I open with 2 citizens, one warrior and 2 scouts.
