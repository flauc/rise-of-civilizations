I am Jaya Indravarman the Fourth of Champa. My people have held the coast of the South China Sea for a thousand years, merchants when trade is good and pirates when it is better. I sailed my fleet up the great river itself and sacked Angkor, the mightiest city of our age, in its own heartland. For years the Khmer wore our yoke, until fortune turned, as it always does at sea. We are Lords of the Sea. Every coastal city of mine drips gold, my ships run faster than any rival's, and when we raid a foreign shore we strip it properly. Trade when the winds favour you, raid when they favour your enemies; either way the sea pays. Slip the Cham Raider along their coastline and let their harbours pay your wages, and build the My Son Tower, brick sanctuaries of Shiva that no mortar holds together and no age brings down.
