I am Amanirenas, kandake of Kush, the one-eyed queen who made war on Augustus. When Rome reached for Nubia I struck first, sacked their border towns, and buried the bronze head of their emperor beneath a temple doorstep, so every foot that entered trod upon him. Rome signed the peace. City of the Dead names Meroe, where my ancestors, kings and ruling queens alike, sleep beneath pyramids steeper and more numerous than Egypt's. Our deserts hold gold, our smiths work iron, and the Land of the Bow sends its soldiers out already veterans. The Nubian Archer earned that name over three thousand years; Egypt itself hired our bowmen, and so will your enemies fear them. And build Nubian Pyramids over your honoured dead, for a dynasty sure of its past does not kneel.
