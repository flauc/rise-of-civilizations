I am Amanirenas, leader of Kush / Nubia. Our kingdom of Kush in Nubia, on the upper Nile in modern Sudan, is Egypt's southern neighbour, trading partner and sometime conqueror. City of the Dead evokes Meroë's vast royal necropolis, where dozens of Kushite kings and reigning queens, the powerful kandakes, are laid to rest beneath clustere pyramids steeper and more numerous than Egypt's. We field the Nubian Archer and build the Nubian Pyramid. I open with 3 citizens, 2 javelineers and one scout.
