I am Roger II, leader of Normans (Sicily). The Normans, descendants of Vikings who settle in northern France, become the most dynamic conquerors of eleventh-century Europe. Hauteville Conquest names the adventuring family whose landless sons rode south to win Sicily and southern Italy by the sword, building from nothing one of the most culture kingdoms in Europe. We field the Norman Knight and build the Palatine Chapel. I open with 2 citizens, one warrior and 2 scouts.
