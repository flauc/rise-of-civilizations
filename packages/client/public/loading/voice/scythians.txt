I am Tomyris, queen of the Massagetae, mother of the Scythian steppe. Cyrus of Persia, conqueror of half the world, took my son by a trick, so I broke his army, and the legend says I gave his severed head its fill of blood. Let every empire hear it: the grass sea has a queen, and she does not forgive. People of the Steppe is our whole way of life. We ride before we walk and shoot before we speak; my cavalry strike harder and range farther than any rival's, and the gold of the burial mounds enriches the tribes. No city binds us, so no city can be taken from us. Loose the Scythian Horse Archer in circling swarms that sting and fade before the enemy can answer, and raise Kurgans over your honoured dead, mounds of gold that make the steppe itself remember its princes.
