People of the Steppe names the entire nomadic way of life that make the Scythians who we are, living on horseback, following the herds, and turning the mounte bow into a weapon of war. We field the Scythian Horse Archer and build the Kurgan. I open with 2 citizens, one warrior and 2 scouts.
