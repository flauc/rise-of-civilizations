I am Askia, leader of Songhai. We Empire, centre on Gao and the great trading city of Timbuktu, become the largest state in West African history under Sunni Ali and Askia Muhammad in the fifteenth and sixteenth centuries. River of Gold reflects Songhai's mastery of the Niger, the great waterway that is at once our highway, our larder and the axis of our trade. We field the Songhai Cavalry and build the River Port. I open with 2 citizens, one warrior and 2 scouts.
