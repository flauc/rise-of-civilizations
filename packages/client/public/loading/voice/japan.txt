I am Tokugawa Ieyasu. Nobunaga hammered the rice cake, Hideyoshi kneaded it, and I, who knew how to wait, ate it. At Sekigahara I ended the age of warring provinces, and my house gave Japan two and a half centuries of peace, ruled from Edo while the emperor reigned in Kyoto. Patience is also a weapon. Mine cut deepest of all. Bushido is the way of the warrior: honour, loyalty, and the acceptance of death before disgrace. My swordsmen strike harder than any rival's, and every recruit musters with a spirit already tempered, for the samurai arts of tea, verse and sword all sharpen the same soul. The Samurai is the finest blade ever fielded; do not waste him on skirmishes. Build the Tenshu Castle, and let its white keep teach every neighbour the price of ambition.
