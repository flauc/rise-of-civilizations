I am Tokugawa, leader of Japan. We develope a distinctive island civilization under emperor and, in practice, the shogun who hold real power, dominate for centuries by the samurai warrior class. Bushido, 'the way of the warrior', is the code of honour, loyalty, courage and self-discipline that governe the life and death of the samurai. We field the Samurai and build the Tenshu Castle. I open with 2 citizens, 2 warriors and one scout.
