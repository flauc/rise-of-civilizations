I am Agamemnon, leader of Mycenaean Greece. We dominate mainland Greece in the late Bronze Age, from roughly 1600 to 1100 BCE, a warlike society of warrior-kings ruling from massive citadels at Mycenae, Tiryns and Pylos. Heroic Age evokes the era the Greeks themselves looke back to as the time of demigods and champions, Agamemnon, Achilles, Ajax, whose deeds Homer would sing centuries later. We field the Mycenaean Spearman and build the Megaron. I open with 2 citizens, 2 warriors and one scout.
