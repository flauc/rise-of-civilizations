I am Andrea Doria, leader of Genoa. We is Venice's great maritime and commercial rival, our merchants, bankers and crossbowmen active from the Black Sea to the Atlantic. Bank of San Giorgio names one of the earliest public banks in the world, founde in 1407 to manage Genoa's state debt and grown so powerful that it administere colonies and revenues in the republic's name. We field the Genoese Crossbowman and build the Banco. I open with 2 citizens, 2 javelineers and one scout.
