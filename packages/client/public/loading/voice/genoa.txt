I am Andrea Doria, admiral of Genoa. For forty years I sold my galleys to kings, to France, to the Emperor, to whoever paid in good coin, and then I sailed home and set my republic free without taking a crown for myself. My city's merchants reach from the Black Sea to the Atlantic, and our bankers hold half the crowned heads of Europe in debt. The Bank of San Giorgio is our true sovereign. Founded to manage the state's debt, it grew so mighty that it governed whole colonies in the republic's name. It swells my treasury beyond any rival's, and every trade route I run pays its quiet tribute to the counting house. Plant my Genoese Crossbowmen behind their great shields and let any assault bleed itself white, and raise a Banco in every city, for gold, wisely lent, wins wars that fleets cannot finish.
