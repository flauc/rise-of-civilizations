I am Mithridates, leader of Parthia. We rise from the northeastern Iranian steppe to rule Persia and Mesopotamia from 247 BCE to 224 CE, governing a feudal realm from Ctesiphon on the Tigris. Parthian Shot names the tactic that make them legendary: a horse archer would feign flight and then twist back in the saddle at full gallop to loose a deadly arrow into his pursuers. We field the Parthian Horse Archer and build the Caravanserai. I open with 2 citizens, one warrior and 2 scouts.
