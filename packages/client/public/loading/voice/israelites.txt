I am Solomon, son of David, king in Jerusalem. When the Lord offered me anything, I asked for wisdom, and everything else followed: fleets sailing to Ophir, cedar from Tyre, kings and queens at my gate bearing riddles and gifts. I once judged between two mothers with a single sword, and all Israel saw whose wisdom was in me. Kingdom of David is our inheritance: one people, one covenant, one city on the hill. Faith gathers in Jerusalem the way waters gather in the sea, culture and gold beside it, and every trade route carries a blessing home. We are small among empires, yet what is written in the heart cannot be burned. The Gibborim, my mighty men, guard what David won. And build the First Temple; I spent seven years raising mine, and no work of my hands mattered more.
