I am Pachacuti, leader of Inca. We build the largest empire of pre-Columbian America, stretching for thousands of miles along the spine of the Andes from our capital Cusco. Mit'a is the rotating labour service every Inca community owe the state, a tax paid not in money but in work, which build the roads, terraces, bridges and storehouses that hold Our empire together. We field the Warak'aq and build the Terrace Farm. I open with 3 citizens, 2 warak'aqs and one scout.
