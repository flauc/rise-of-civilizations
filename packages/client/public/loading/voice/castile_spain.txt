I am Isabella of Castile. I took a broken inheritance, married Ferdinand of Aragon, and made of two crowns one Spain. In the year Granada fell I ended a struggle of seven centuries, and in that same year I staked my treasure on a Genoese sailor's westward gamble. He found a new world, and it answered to me. El Escorial is the soul of my Spain set in stone: palace, monastery and tomb in one, gold and faith fused into power. Silver fills my treasury, zeal steels my soldiers, and the cities I take I do not raze; I settle them, convert them, and keep their people as my own. Send the Conquistadors where the maps go blank; a handful of them can topple thrones. And plant the Mission beside every conquest, for a land is only truly won when it prays with you.
