I am Werner Stauffacher, leader of Swiss Confederacy. We began as an alliance of Alpine cantons that win our freedom from Habsburg overlordship and defende it in a series of stunning victories over far grander feudal armies. Reisläufer names the Swiss mercenaries who march out from our mountain cantons to sell the services of our devastating pike squares to the powers of Europe. We field the Swiss Halberdier and build the Rütli Meadow. I open with 2 citizens, 2 warriors and one scout.
