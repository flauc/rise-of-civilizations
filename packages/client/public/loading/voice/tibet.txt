I am Songtsen Gampo, who gathered the clans of the high plateau into one Tibetan Empire. My horsemen rode down from the roof of the world until even Tang China treated with us, and my brides from Nepal and China carried the Buddha's teaching into my court. I gave my people a script, a law, and Lhasa itself. Roof of the World is our fortress and our altar. The thin air that turns armies back breeds a hardy people: faith rises from the monasteries, culture rises with it, my riders fight the stronger, and my armies cross the mountains as easily as other men cross meadows. My Tibetan Cavalry strike where no lowland army believes a horse can go. And raise the Potala above your capital, palace and temple in one, standing closer to heaven than any rival's walls.
