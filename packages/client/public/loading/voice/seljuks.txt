I am Alp Arslan, leader of Seljuks. The Seljuk Turks swept out of Central Asia in the eleventh century to conquer Persia and the Near East, becoming champions of Sunni Islam and protectors of the Abbasid caliph. Ghazi names the Muslim frontier warriors who wage holy war on the borders of Islam, drawn by faith and plunder to the edges of Byzantine Anatolia. We field the Ghulam and build the Madrasa. I open with 2 citizens, one warrior and 2 scouts.
