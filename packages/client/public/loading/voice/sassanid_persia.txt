I am Khosrow, leader of Sassanid Persia. The Sassanid Empire revive Persian glory from 224 to 651 CE, casting itself as heir to the Achaemenids and making Zoroastrianism a state religion. Eranshahr, 'the realm of the Iranians', is the Sassanids' own name for our empire, a deliberate assertion that we are the rightful, divinely sanctione heirs of Cyrus and Darius. We field the Savaran Cataphract and build the Fire Temple. I open with 2 citizens, one warrior and 2 scouts.
