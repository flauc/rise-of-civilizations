I am Periander, leader of Corinth. We commande the narrow isthmus linking the Peloponnese to mainland Greece, controlling traffic between two seas and growing into one of the wealthiest commercial cities of the Greek world. Our Two Seas: Coastal cities +3 gold; +3 gold per trade route; naval units +1 movement. We field the Corinthian Trireme and build the Diolkos. I open with 2 citizens, one warrior and 2 scouts.
