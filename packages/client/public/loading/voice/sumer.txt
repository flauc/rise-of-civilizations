I am Gilgamesh of Uruk, the king who saw the deep. Between the Tigris and the Euphrates my people raise the first cities the world has ever known: Ur, Uruk, Eridu, Lagash. We cut canals through the marsh, harness the wheel and the plough, and tally grain on wet clay, and in those small pressed marks we invent writing itself. Every city that will ever stand begins here, in our mud and reed. I crossed mountains and oceans hunting eternal life, and I came home with something better: knowledge, and walls to carve it on. That is our Epic Quest. Sumer builds quicker and learns quicker than any rival, for the temple feeds the labourer and the school shapes the scribe. Send my War-Carts thundering across the floodplain before the enemy raises a single wall, and set a Ziggurat in every city, so heaven itself watches over our work.
