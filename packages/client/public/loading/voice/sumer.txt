I am Gilgamesh, leader of Sumer. In the marshy floodplain between the Tigris and Euphrates, the Sumerians build the world's first cities in the late fourth millennium BCE, Ur, Uruk, Eridu and Lagash. Epic Quest takes our name from the Epic of Gilgamesh, the world's oldest surviving great poem, set down in Sumerian and later Akkadian on clay tablets. We field the War-Cart and build the Ziggurat. I open with 3 citizens, one warrior and 2 scouts.
