I am Untash-Napirisha, king of Anshan and Susa. Elam has watched Mesopotamia from the mountains for two thousand years, speaking a tongue no neighbour understands, writing in a script all our own. We raided their plains and carried home their treasures; Hammurabi's own law stone stands in Susa as our trophy. Mine are the Highland Archers, bred in the Zagros where every boy shoots before he can plough. Rivals pay any price for Susian bowmen; mine march out already blooded, and literate Susa, old as writing itself, keeps my scholars ahead of every court. Loose the Susian Archer from the high ground and no line will ever reach you; feather them at range and let the mountains finish the work. And raise Chogha Zanbil, the great ziggurat I built for the gods, so they remember who honoured them first.
