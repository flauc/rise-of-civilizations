I am Untash, leader of Elam. Elam, centre on Susa in the lowlands and Anshan in the highlands of southwest Iran, is Mesopotamia's ancient eastern neighbour and rival for well over two millennia. Highland Archers reflects the Elamites' fame as bowmen, drawn from the rug Zagros where hunting and raiding make the bow a way of life. We field the Susian Archer and build the Chogha Zanbil. I open with 2 citizens, 2 javelineers and one scout.
