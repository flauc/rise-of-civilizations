I am Cocijo priesthood, leader of Zapotec. We build one of Mesoamerica's earliest cities at Monte Albán, dramatically site atop a level mountain in the Oaxaca valley, around 500 BCE. Cloud People (Be'ena'a) is the Zapotecs' own name for themselves, a people who hold we have sprung from the clouds and rocks of our mountain homeland rather than migrating from elsewhere. We field the Zapotec Warrior and build the Danzante Temple. I open with 2 citizens, 2 warriors and one scout.
