I am Ezana of Aksum, king of kings. From the highlands I command the Red Sea, the hinge between Rome, Arabia and India, and through my port of Adulis pass ivory, incense and gold. I struck my own gold coinage, and I was among the first kings on earth to take the Christian faith. Red Sea Trade is Aksum's station in the world. A Persian prophet counted us among the four great powers of the earth, and he was right to. My coastal cities grow rich on every sail that passes, and the new faith knits the kingdom together. The Aksumite Spearman holds the highland passes no invader has ever kept; let the mountains fight beside him. And raise the Stelae, towers of carved granite above the royal tombs, taller than anything Rome ever cut from a single stone, so the world may read our permanence.
