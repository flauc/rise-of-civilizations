I am Minchansaman, last great king of Chimor. My capital Chan Chan is the largest adobe city ever raised, ten walled palaces on a coast where rain almost never falls. We led rivers out of the mountains through canals and made the desert bloom, and our goldsmiths had no equals in all the Andes. Kingdom of Chimor is that mastery of the impossible coast. Gold and craft pour from my workshops, and cities set in the desert feed and pay as if they stood in a green valley. When the Inca finally overcame us, they marched my smiths to Cuzco to teach them; even conquest bowed to our craft. The Chimu Slinger greets invaders with stones from beyond spear reach; screen your armies with him. And build the Chan Chan Citadel, walled compound of kings, so what the goldsmiths make, the walls keep. In the desert, water is the treasury behind the treasury.
