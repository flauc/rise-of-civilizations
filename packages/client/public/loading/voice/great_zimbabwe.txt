I am Nyatsimba, leader of Great Zimbabwe. We is the stone-build capital of a powerful Shona kingdom that flourish between the eleventh and fifteenth centuries on the plateau between the Zambezi and Limpopo rivers. Cattle & Stone names the twin pillars of Our kingdom's power. We field the Zimbabwe Spearman and build the Great Enclosure. I open with 2 citizens, 2 warriors and one scout.
