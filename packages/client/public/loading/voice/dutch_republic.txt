I am William the Silent, leader of Dutch Republic. We emerge from a long revolt against Habsburg Spain and, in our seventeenth-century Golden Age, lead the world in trade, finance and seafaring. Grachten, the canals threading every Dutch city, stands for a people who build our prosperity by mastering the water: draining marshes, ringing fields with dykes, and turning canals into the highways of trade. We field the Sea Beggar and build the Polder. I open with 2 citizens, one warrior and 2 scouts.
