I am Le Loi, leader of Dai Viet (Vietnam). Dai Viet is the resilient Vietnamese state of the Red River delta that, again and again, threw off domination by our giant northern neighbour. Nine Dragons names the nine-mouthe delta of the Mekong (Cuu Long), emblem of the rivers and rice paddies that are the heartland and the refuge of the Vietnamese people. We field the Voi Chiến and build the Thành. I open with 3 citizens, one warrior and 2 scouts.
