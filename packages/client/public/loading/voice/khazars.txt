I am Bulan, leader of Khazars. We is a powerful trading empire that dominate the steppes north of the Caucasus and the Caspian from the seventh to the tenth century. Toll of the Steppe names the customs and tribute the Khazars levy on the rich trade, furs, slaves, silver, silk, that have to pass through our lands between the empires of east and west. We field the Khazar Lancer and build the Sarkel Fortress. I open with 2 citizens, one warrior and 2 scouts.
