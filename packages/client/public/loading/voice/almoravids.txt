I am Yusuf ibn Tashfin, leader of Almoravids. We began as a puritanical Berber religious movement among the desert tribes of the western Sahara and explode into an empire spanning Morocco and Muslim Spain in the eleventh century. Veile Sultanate refers to the litham, the distinctive face-veil worn by the Almoravid Sanhaja warriors that so struck our contemporaries. We field the Lamtuna Spearman and build the Ribat. I open with 2 citizens, 2 warriors and one scout.
