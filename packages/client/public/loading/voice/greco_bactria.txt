I am Demetrius I, leader of Greco-Bactria. The Greco-Bactrian kingdom arose when the easternmost provinces of Alexander's empire, in what is now Afghanistan and Central Asia, broke away under our Greek governors. Thousand Cities is the ancient epithet for Bactria, a byword for how prosperous and densely urbanise this fertile crossroads have become under Greek rule. We field the Bactrian Cataphract and build the Gymnasion. I open with 2 citizens, one warrior and 2 scouts.
