I am Krum, leader of Bulgaria. We is founde when Khan Asparuh's Bulgars, a steppe people, settle south of the Danube among the Slavs in the seventh century. Khans of the Danube names the Bulgar rulers, Asparuh, Krum, Simeon, who carve out and defende a realm south of the great river against the full might of Byzantium, at times turning the tables and besieging Constantinople itself. We field the Bulgar Horse Archer and build the Preslav Court. I open with 2 citizens, one warrior and 2 scouts.
