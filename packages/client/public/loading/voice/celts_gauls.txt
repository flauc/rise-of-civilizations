I am Vercingetorix, leader of Celts / Gauls. We spread across much of Iron Age Europe, from Gaul and Britain to central Anatolia, a patchwork of warlike tribes unite by language, art and the learned priestly caste of the druids. Druidic Lore reflects the druids, the priests, judges, healers and keepers of memory who hold Celtic society together where there is little writing. We field the Gaesatae and build the Oppidum. I open with 2 citizens, 2 warriors and one scout.
