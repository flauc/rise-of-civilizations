I am Nearchus, leader of Crete. Long after the fall of our Minoan palaces, the island of Crete is fame throughout the Greek and Hellenistic world for one thing above all: our archers. Cretan Archers names the island's celebrate mercenary bowmen, so skilled that no Hellenistic or Roman army of note march without them. We field the Cretan Archer and build the Gortyn Code. I open with 2 citizens, 2 javelineers and one scout.
