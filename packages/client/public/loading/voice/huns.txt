I am Attila, leader of Huns. We swept out of the Eurasian steppe into Europe in the fourth and fifth centuries, our arrival driving other peoples before them and helping to topple the Western Roman Empire. Scourge of God is the name terrify Christians give Attila, seeing in his devastations the punishment of heaven upon a sinful world. We field the Hunnic Horde and build the Ordu. I open with 2 citizens, one warrior and 2 scouts.
