Delete your account

Rise of Civilizations · Account & data deletion

Who this is for

This page explains how registered players of Rise of Civilizations can delete their account and request removal of associated data we store on our servers.

Guest players (not signed in) do not have a server account. Clear the app’s site data or uninstall the app to remove local saves on your device.

Option 1 — Delete in the app (fastest)

  1. Open Rise of Civilizations and sign in to your account.
  2. On the home screen, tap Settings.
  3. Scroll to Account and tap Delete account.
  4. Enter your password and confirm. Your account is removed immediately from our servers.

Option 2 — Request by email

If you cannot access the app, email legal@rise-of-civilizations.com from the address linked to your account, or include your username (handle). We may ask you to verify your identity before deleting the account.

What we delete

  • Your registered account (username / handle)
  • Password hash and active login sessions
  • Email address stored for newsletter opt-in (if any)

What may be kept

  • Single-player saves on your device — stored locally until you clear app data or uninstall; they are not uploaded to our servers by default.
  • Analytics and bug reports — may remain in aggregated or pseudonymous form for operations and security, as described in our Privacy Policy.
  • Backups and legal records — limited retention where required by law or for abuse investigations.

How long it takes

In-app deletion is immediate. Email requests are usually processed within 30 days.

More information
