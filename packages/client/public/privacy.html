Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 13, 2026 · Rise of Civilizations

1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy explains how Rise of Civilizations ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our game, websites, and multiplayer services (the "Service").

Contact us via our Support form — available on the home screen and in the footer inside the game, or at /support in your browser.

2. What we collect

DataWhenPurpose
Username (handle) Account registration Identify your account in multiplayer and on leaderboards
Password Registration / login Stored only as a one-way hash (bcrypt); we never store plain-text passwords
Email address Optional, if you opt in to newsletter Send news and notifications you requested

Gameplay analytics While you play (online or when events are sent) Session start/end, game mode, turns played, outcome, civilization chosen, client version

Server logs When you connect IP address, timestamps, and connection metadata for security and operations

We do not sell your personal data. We do not show third-party advertising in the Game.

3. Legal basis (EEA / UK users)

Where GDPR applies, we process data on these bases:

  • Contract — to provide accounts and multiplayer you request;
  • Consent — for optional newsletter email;
  • Legitimate interests — analytics, security, and improving the Game, balanced against your rights;
  • Legal obligation — when we must retain or disclose data by law.

4. How we use data

  • Operate accounts, authentication, and multiplayer lobbies;
  • Save game progress for registered users where that feature is enabled;
  • Understand how the Game is used and fix bugs;
  • Send newsletter emails only if you opted in;
  • Enforce our Terms of Service and protect the Service from abuse.

5. Where data is stored

Account and analytics data are stored on servers we operate (currently in-memory or database storage on infrastructure we control). Data may be processed in the European Union or other countries where our hosting providers operate. We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect data.

6. Retention

  • Account data — kept while your account is active; deleted or anonymized after a deletion request, subject to backup and legal retention limits;
  • Analytics — retained in aggregated or pseudonymous form for operational and statistical purposes;
  • Server logs — typically rotated within a limited period unless needed for security investigations;
  • Local saves — remain on your device until you clear site data or uninstall the app.

7. Sharing

We may share data only:

  • with infrastructure providers that host our servers, under confidentiality obligations;
  • with app store operators (Google, Apple) as required by their platforms when you install mobile builds;
  • when required by law or to protect rights, safety, and security;
  • in connection with a merger or acquisition, with notice where required.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or export your personal data, object to or restrict certain processing, and withdraw consent (for example, newsletter opt-in).

To exercise these rights, use our Support form (choose Privacy or data request) from the home screen or footer inside the game, or at /support. Include your username and, if possible, the email linked to your account. We may need to verify your identity before responding.

EEA residents may lodge a complaint with their local data-protection authority. In Croatia, this is the Croatian Personal Data Protection Agency (AZOP).

9. Children

The Service is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us through the Support form and we will delete it.

10. Cookies and local storage

The browser client uses localStorage and IndexedDB for session tokens, analytics queues, client IDs, and single-player saves. We do not use third-party advertising cookies. Mobile apps use equivalent on-device storage.

11. International transfers

If data is transferred outside the EEA, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses or adequacy decisions where applicable.

12. Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Continued use after changes means you accept the updated policy.

13. Contact

Questions, privacy requests, and other help: use our Support form at /support. It is available from the home screen and footer while you are in the game. Choose the inquiry type that best matches your question (for privacy and data rights, select Privacy or data request).
